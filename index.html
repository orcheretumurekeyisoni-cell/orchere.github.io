My Entrepreneurial Journey

My journey began in ENT 100 and ENT 110, where I discovered the power of innovation in solving community challenges. I chose to address healthcare access issues in Rwanda and developed skills in design thinking, empathy, and leadership.

My Solution

I created a mobile platform that connects rural patients with clinics and health volunteers. It supports appointment booking, health education in Kinyarwanda, and remote consultations.

Prototype

The prototype shows how users can locate clinics and schedule visits easily. It features a simple, multilingual interface.
